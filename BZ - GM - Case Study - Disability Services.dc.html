The challenge

National Disability Services (NDS) is Australia's peak body for non‑government disability service organisations. Collectively in Western Australia, approximately 80 NDS (WA) members operate several hundred services for Western Australians with all types of disability, ranging in size from small support groups to large multi‑service organisations.

NDS (WA)'s purpose is to promote quality service provision and life opportunities for Western Australians with disability — achieved by influencing governments at all levels and providing members with information and advice, networking opportunities and business support.

Using Governance Manager

Sector governance arrangements are under strict scrutiny from a broad range of stakeholders. Significant governance failings attract media interest, and issues impacting one service provider may have a carryover effect across the entire sector. Good governance is the lynchpin for confidence — supporting sound management practices, robust policy suites, and efficient performance in serving clients and other stakeholders.

In 2011, with funding provided by the Disability Services Commission, the tool was adopted for use in WA. A panel of six sector agencies used the final system and provided positive feedback on its relevance for CEOs and Boards of Management. Governance Manager is offered as a value‑added service to members, enabling providers and their boards to identify current capability and set goals across boards and committees, financial management, ethics and integrity, social capital, people management, risk management and communications — with a reporting mechanism and tracked “agreed actions”.

Outcome achieved
  • Well‑governed service providers, with greater transparency and competency sector wide
  • Demonstrated leadership through innovative, value‑added services that assist members
  • Whole‑of‑sector oversight of governance strengths and weaknesses

Applicable where a peak body wants to offer members a genuine value-added service while gaining whole-of-sector oversight of governance strengths and weaknesses.
