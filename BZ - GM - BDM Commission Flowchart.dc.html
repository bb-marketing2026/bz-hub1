Service delivery

Owns the commission register

1Sale attributed

Recorded only where the BDM personally generated the sale. A closed deal alone is not commissionable.

→
2Invoices raised

Track invoice numbers, dates and gross invoiced revenue against the sale, by calendar month.

→
3First invoice paidGATE

Record the payment date. Until the first invoice is paid, no commission is earned — carry the sale forward.

→
4SDM calculates

5% of gross sales revenue invoiced for that sale in the period. Base × 5% = commission claimed.

▼
Handoff · Monthly schedule by cut-off

Employee and calculation month · deal reference confirmed as personally generated · invoice numbers, dates and gross revenue · first-invoice payment date and evidence · base × 5% · any exception needing approval.

Finance

Validates the commercial data

5Finance validates independently

Attribution — the sale was personally generated by the BDM.

Invoice values, and that the first invoice was actually paid by the cut-off.

The SDM calculation, then sends the approved amount to Payroll.

What the base is

Invoiced revenue. Not contracted deal value, and not cash collections beyond the first-invoice trigger.

▼
Handoff · Payroll input

Approved commission amount for the month · guarantee top-up if applicable · approved integrity deduction with authorisation · the pay period it is processed in.

Payroll

Pays the approved entitlement

6Payroll pays next month

Commission earned in a calendar month is paid in the first pay period of the following month. Any integrity-related deduction is separately approved before Payroll action — it is not a routine clawback.

September 2026 only

First-month guarantee: total pay is the higher of PHP 120,000 or base salary plus commission earned for the Guarantee Period. It does not recur.

Exceptions and deviations
First invoice not paid by cut-off

No commission is earned yet. Carry the sale forward until the trigger occurs.

SDM tracks · Finance validates the payment date

September 2026 guarantee

Pay the higher of PHP 120,000 total pay, or base salary plus commission earned during the Guarantee Period.

Finance calculates · Payroll processes any top-up

Later cancellation, refund or non-payment

No clawback of properly earned commission. The only exception is a refund caused by a clear integrity issue attributable to the employee.
