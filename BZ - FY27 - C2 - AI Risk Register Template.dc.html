Ten AI risks are recorded; after current controls, one remains extreme and five high. The extreme risk — unmanaged "shadow" AI use — has no formal owner or inventory. The register recommends three priority treatments this quarter: an AI use inventory, a named accountable owner per system, and an ISO/IEC 42001-aligned policy.

Ratings use a 5×5 likelihood × consequence matrix. Inherent = before controls; residual = after current controls. Cell markers show residual position.
