Commercial terms

Duration. 7-day access supports a single point-in-time assessment; 12-month access permits assessment, action and re-assessment within the period. Access commences on framework deployment.

Improvements. Where 'assessment only' is selected, the improvement planning module may be added later at the rate quoted, for the remainder of the access period.

Validity. This quotation is valid for 30 days from the date of issue.

Payment. Invoiced on commencement, payable within 30 days. Multi‑year access periods may be invoiced annually in advance.
