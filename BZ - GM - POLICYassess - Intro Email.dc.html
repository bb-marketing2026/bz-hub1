Most governance and corporate services leaders I speak with can produce a policy register on request. Far fewer can produce evidence that the framework is actually working — how much of the library is current, who owns each document after the last restructure, whether staff understand the policies that apply to them, and whether practice matches what the policy says.

That isn't a criticism of anyone's library. It's usually a resourcing problem, and often one you've already raised more than once.

The POLICYassess Framework in Governance Manager measures policy governance across four domains: policy development and clarity, compliance and alignment, engagement and implementation, and monitoring and improvement. Each is scored for maturity and for how readily it could be evidenced — which is the distinction an auditor cares about.

I'd like to offer you a free assessment against one domain of the framework — whichever you choose. Practically:

  • Under an hour of your time, on the single domain you're least comfortable about
  • A maturity profile on that domain and a prioritised gap list, in writing
  • A 30‑minute debrief with me to talk through what it shows
  • The result is yours — nothing goes to your executive unless you send it

Two places it tends to earn its hour quickly: the next internal audit that tests policy currency, and the next time you need to make the resourcing case for policy work with something more than an assertion behind it.

Worth a short call to see whether it's useful? If the timing's wrong, I'm happy to just send the framework overview so you have it when the audit cycle comes around.
