Product design specification · BZ‑CUF‑001

Butterfly cufflinks

A pair of crisp, circular die‑struck cufflinks built from the Blue Zoo origami butterfly mark. Raised polished‑metal lines hold the mark's straight‑edged facets; hard enamel carries the two brand hues — deep navy and teal — so the triangles read cleanly across a boardroom table. Considered, understated, unmistakably us.

1  Overview & intent

These cufflinks are a staff and partner keepsake — worn with a business shirt. They should read instantly as Blue Zoo, feel premium and weighted in the hand, and fasten easily through a double or single cuff. The origami butterfly is a solid, two‑tone geometric mark, so a die‑struck hard‑enamel build suits it far better than a cut outline: raised polished‑metal ridges separate each facet and hold the enamel, keeping the mark's crisp, straight‑edged geometry honest. The pair is supplied identical, with the mark upright on both.

The mark is used exactly as supplied — the origami butterfly, symbol only, no wordmark. The facet geometry, the navy/teal split and the relative proportions must not be altered. This document is the single source of truth for the manufacturer; anything not drawn here is to be confirmed with Blue Zoo before tooling.

2  Construction

Each cufflink is a single die‑struck circular face with baked hard enamel, joined to a swivel‑bar fitting on the rear.

  • Face — die‑struck brass. The mark is struck into a Ø 16 mm round face of 1.5 mm jewellers' brass so each facet sits in its own recessed cell, divided by raised metal ridges ~0.25 mm wide. Ridges and any exposed metal are polished silver plated.
  • Colour — hard enamel. Each cell is hand‑filled with hard (cloisonné‑style) enamel in the specified navy or teal, slightly over‑filled, then lapped flat and polished level with the metal ridges for a smooth, durable, glass‑like face.
  • Fitting. A soldered swivel‑bar (whale‑back) toggle on the rear folds flat to pass through the cuff, then pivots to lock. The whole face is polished flush; metal ridges read as bright silver lines between flat navy and teal facets. No epoxy dome.

4  Colour reproduction

The mark uses the two Blue Zoo brand hues — deep navy for the leading facets and teal for the trailing facets. Match to Pantone; the enamel proof must be approved against a physical chip before the run.

Unlike a printed asset, each cufflink carries two flat enamel colours only — the navy/teal relationship reads through the hard split at the metal ridge, not a gradient. Keep facets solid; do not blend colours within a cell.

5  Rear & fitting

  • Swivel‑bar fitting (whale‑back) centred on the rear; folds flat to pass the buttonhole, then pivots 90° to lock.
  • Bridge / stem soldered to the face at ≥ 3 kg pull strength; smooth, rattle‑free pivot with no sharp edges against the cuff.
  • Backstamp laser‑etched into the rear of each face: the lowercase bluezoo wordmark. No ink.
  • Supplied as a matched pair, both faces identical with the mark upright.

6  Packaging & presentation

Each pair ships in a hinged gift box (85 × 55 × 32 mm) with a navy‑lined foam bed holding both cufflinks, the butterfly mark foil‑stamped on the lid and a single line of copy — “Steady counsel, worn with pride.” — inside. Box wrapped in soft‑touch navy stock, 350 gsm, subtle corners to echo the mark's straight edges.

Use the full primary lockup on packaging; the cufflink faces stay purely the butterfly mark, no wordmark.
