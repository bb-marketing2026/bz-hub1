BDM weekly cadence

The operating rhythm that delivers 37.6 sales hours a week and 400 new prospect contacts a month

Sales works best in the morning. The day starts at 8:30am, and the first hour is fixed — it is the only part of the week that is not negotiable, because it is the part that produces everything downstream. The remainder of the day sits within the BDM's own locus of control.

Section 1

The daily rhythm

8:3015 min
HubSpot maintenance

Three checks, every morning, before anything else: no deals missing information, no deals with more than 14 days inactivity, no deals without a Next Step. Fix what fails — do not note it for later.

8:4545 min
Prospecting block

Reach‑outs, meeting requests, follow‑up emails and calls. New‑for‑New and New‑for‑Existing focus only — no Existing‑for‑Existing work in this block. Roughly 20 new contacts logged with outreach performed.

9:30to 12:30
Prime selling window

Connected calls, scheduled meetings, platform demonstrations and executive debriefs. Book meetings into this window by preference — senior people take calls in the morning. Mondays start with the 15‑minute sales team update at 9:30.

Afternoonflexible
Assessments, proposals, close

Assessment facilitation, profile production, proposal scoping in PandaDoc, negotiation and follow‑through. Within the BDM's locus of control — sequence it as the pipeline demands.

List building6+ hrs/wk
List building & contact scraping

At least six hours a week building the target lists the prospecting block draws on — agency directories, annual reports, org charts, LinkedIn and association member registers. Cross‑checked, de‑duplicated and loaded into HubSpot against the active campaign cohort.

Day end10 min
Log and set up tomorrow

Record outcomes and hours in HubSpot and Xero. Every deal touched today leaves with a Next Step and a date.

37.6 hrs

sales hours logged per week. Non‑negotiable. A short week is raised with the sponsor in advance, not explained afterwards.

100

new contacts logged per week with initial outreach performed — roughly 20 per prospecting block, 400 per month.

15

connected calls per week. Decision‑maker conversations, tracked in HubSpot irrespective of inbound volume.

6 hrs

list building per week. Minimum. Without a built list the prospecting block has nothing to work from.
