You'll have had plenty of emails about telematics, fuel cards and leasing. This isn't one of them.

After a serious fleet incident — or an adverse audit finding — the questions that follow are rarely about maintenance records. They're governance questions. Who authorised that replacement, and on what basis? How is utilisation actually measured? Where is the evidence that compliance obligations were being tracked, not assembled afterwards? Was the fleet strategy aligned to anything the executive had approved?

Most Fleet Managers I speak with are confident the answers are sound. Very few can produce them in a form an auditor, an insurer or a chief executive would accept — because nobody has ever measured fleet governance as a thing in its own right.

That's what the FLEETassess Framework in Governance Manager does. It assesses fleet governance across four capabilities — strategic alignment, regulatory compliance, lifecycle management and data analytics — and produces a maturity profile with a ranked list of the gaps that matter most. You don't need to take on all four — pick the one you're least comfortable about and we'll assess just that. It doesn't replace your fleet management system; it reads the governance wrapped around it.

I'd like to offer you a free assessment against one domain of the framework — whichever you choose. Practically:

  • Under an hour of your time, no data extracts required — one domain, your pick
  • A maturity profile on that domain and a prioritised gap list, in writing
  • A 30‑minute debrief with me to talk through what it shows
  • The result is yours — nothing goes to your executive unless you send it

Two places it tends to earn its hour quickly: the next insurance renewal, and the next time fleet is asked to absorb a budget cut without an evidence base to argue from.

Worth a short call to see whether it's useful? If the timing's wrong, I'm happy to just send the framework overview so you have it when you need it.
