Who we are

Vision, mission & positioning

Blue Zoo advises boards and executives across Governance, Strategy, Risk and Resilience, backed by its proprietary platform, Governance Manager. We see things differently — senior counsel and specialist focus, without the big-firm price tag.

Vision

Steady growth over the next three to five years toward a fully managed model — allowing the Managing Director to retire while the business continues to deliver a dividend stream for shareholders.

Mission

Make the complexities of governance and strategy simple — fit-for-purpose advice that builds internal capability and leaves organisations stronger and self-sufficient.

Value

We live the Brainbox Group corporate value of 'Always Be Kind' — in how we work with clients, partners and each other.

Why clients choose Blue Zoo
  • Mid-market rates — senior specialists without the global-firm premium.
  • Nearly 20 years active on WA public-sector procurement panels.
  • Well regarded in Western Australia — including landmark work codifying the WA public-sector governance framework.
  • Proprietary technology — Governance Manager, in market since 2009.
  • Practical, not templated — advice tailored to each organisation.

The market we serve

Market & operating environment

Blue Zoo's core market is the Western Australian public sector — state agencies, statutory authorities, local government and their boards. The current environment is unusually favourable for governance advice.

138+

WA local government entities, plus state agencies & authorities

2025–30

OAG strategic plan raising the accountability & performance bar

1 Jul 25

machinery-of-government reform reshaping agencies

Tailwinds
  • Public-sector reform (MoG changes) is disrupting agencies and lifting demand for governance and control uplift.
  • Auditor General focus on cyber, fraud, performance audits and AI governance.
  • Rising board-level demand for AI governance and assurance.
  • Growing appetite for measurable governance maturity and benchmarking.
Headwinds
  • Tight public-sector budgets constrain discretionary advisory spend.
  • Procurement panels (CUAs) gate access and compress rates.
  • Global firms carry brand weight and scale we cannot match.
  • Reform can delay procurement decisions as agencies restructure.

Sources: WA Office of the Auditor General Strategic Plan 2025–30; State & Local Government 2025 audit results; Public Sector Reform (machinery of government), effective 1 July 2025.

SWOT

How we get there

Transformation initiatives

Three transformations underpin the year — reshaping the workforce, lifting capability through AI, and moving sales from reactive to proactive.

01Transform workforce
  • Second Philippines-based Auditor / Advisor in Q2, and an additional Australian-based Advisor in Q4.
  • Start recruitment for the Senior Advisor in July, assuming an extended search for the right resource.
  • Extend Karylle to advisory projects (~25% utilisation on advisory, remainder on GM).
  • Targeted use of casual employees and contractors.
02Transform capability
  • Expand use of AI, with a focus on AI for Consulting and AI for Sales.
  • Increase advisor efficiency, reducing costs on fixed-price projects.
  • Broaden the use of PH-based resources to drive project delivery, not simply support engagements — moving beyond taking notes.
03Transform sales
  • Move sales from reactive to proactive — two sales meetings per week across public and private sector.
  • Increase the size and profitability of engagements.
  • Lift average advisory project size from $25k to $35k, with at least one large project (>$50k) running at any time.
  • Increase the hourly rate for time-and-materials projects by 5%.
  • Maintain sales efficiency by automating proposal development and marketing content generation.

Margin & cash

Keeping the plan alive

Governance, review & the first 90 days

A strategy is only as good as the rhythm that keeps it on track. Blue Zoo governs FY27 through a monthly Board cadence and a clear set of near-term priorities.

Governance & review cadence
  • Monthly Board meeting with a Strategy Progress Report against this plan.
  • Monthly financial review against budget and forecast (Xero).
  • Compliance Register maintained and reviewed at Board.
  • Full strategic plan refreshed each June.
First 90 days — priorities
  • Start Senior Advisor recruitment (July).
  • Onboard the second PH Advisor.
  • Launch the proactive sales cadence (2 meetings/week).
  • Deliver Campaign 1 (performance-audit readiness) in September.
  • Begin VMIA stakeholder engagement ahead of the extension.
In one line

Grow steadily toward a managed model — deeper client relationships, a stronger team, recurring revenue and disciplined governance — so the firm endures beyond any single person or contract.
