Your business driver

Independent, expert support for your CEO review

The Town of Victoria Park seeks to appoint a suitably qualified consultant to provide expert independent advice and guidance to Council to facilitate the review of the Chief Executive Officer’s performance and develop Key Performance Indicators for FY27.. As mandated with the Local Government Act 1995 (section 5.38), the Town of Victoria Park (the Town) requires an annual performance review of the Chief Executive Officer. For the purposes of this annual review, Council seeks to appoint a suitably qualified external consultant to undertake the performance evaluation process of the Chief Executive Officer. The period being assessed is for the probationary period during the financial year 2023-24, and then a substantive review on completion of a cycle yet to be determined by Council. The relationship between a Council and its CEO is fundamental to the successful delivery of Town’s Strategic Community Plan (SCP). The relationship provides the bridge between the SCP and the Corporate Business Plan (CBP) and its supporting Integrated Planning and Reporting Framework (IPRF) documents, taking them from paper to community value. In addition to ‘good practice’, Local Governments have a requirement to carry out CEO reviews on an annual basis, and setting clear objectives and goals are critical to success. These goals and effective evaluation of CEO performance and expectations, helps to mitigate the risk of CEO turnover, and avoid disruption and potential non-compliance for the local government. Using an external and suitably qualified evaluator minimises the risk of governance issues that may generate adverse bad public relations outcomes for the Town. Blue Zoo will ensure that the CEO review process is conducted in accordance with the Town’s existing policies and standards for CEO performance appraisal, whilst ensuring that this is in alignment with the Department of Local Government, Sport and Cultural Industries (DLGSC) framework and guidelines for CEO performance appraisals. The Blue Zoo approach to KPI development has been acknowledged by many local governments as being a clear and pragmatic framework that provides direct linkage between the intent of Council and the activities of its CEO whilst maintaining full alignment with the SCP/CBP and all regulatory obligations. By engaging Blue Zoo, the Town of Victoria Park will access significant previous experience developed when undertaking similar work for Bands 1 to 4 Local Governments, and Blue Zoo looks forward to working with the Town for this engagement.

The process

CEO performance review

CEO Performance Review

Blue Zoo follows a best-practice model for the review of a Chief Executive that has been refined throughout decades of professional practice and many reviews of both Government and Corporate CEOs. It is designed to yield a fair and balanced perspective on the performance of a CEO.

1
Meeting with Mayor and CEO to Discuss Review Process

Blue Zoo begins the performance appraisal process by conducting a meeting with the Mayor and CEO to establish a clear and transparent framework for the review. This initial meeting sets the tone for the entire appraisal, ensuring all parties are aligned on objectives, expectations, and timelines. Blue Zoo facilitates the discussion to explain the methodology and tools that will be used throughout the review, including surveys, performance metrics, and the evaluation of qualitative and quantitative data. During this meeting, Blue Zoo clarifies the roles and responsibilities of the Mayor and CEO in the process. This includes discussing how feedback will be gathered from staff and elected members, how KPIs and behavioural competencies will be evaluated, and how the final report will be structured. Any specific issues or priorities that the council or CEO wishes to address are documented to ensure they are incorporated into the appraisal. The meeting also establishes a collaborative foundation by fostering open communication and trust among the key stakeholders. Blue Zoo ensures that everyone involved has a clear understanding of the appraisal’s purpose, which is to provide an objective and constructive evaluation of the CEO’s performance while identifying opportunities for professional growth. By addressing any concerns or uncertainties upfront, Blue Zoo ensures a smooth and effective appraisal process that is focused on achieving the council’s strategic objectives and supporting the CEO’s leadership. METHODOLOGY

2
FY 2023 Letter Issued to CEO with Invitation to Prepare a Summary Report

Blue Zoo issues a formal letter to the CEO inviting them to prepare a comprehensive summary report of their achievements and challenges over the past year. This step empowers the CEO to contribute actively to their performance appraisal by providing their perspective on how they have fulfilled their responsibilities and delivered on their KPIs. The letter outlines the key elements that the CEO’s summary report should cover, such as progress on strategic objectives, key accomplishments, challenges encountered, and plans for addressing any outstanding issues. By preparing this report, the CEO has the opportunity to reflect on their leadership performance, highlight significant contributions, and provide context for areas where targets may not have been fully met. This report also allows the CEO to share insights into the operational environment, including any external factors that may have influenced outcomes. For instance, the CEO may describe how unforeseen challenges, such as a major infrastructure issue, impacted the achievement of KPIs. Blue Zoo ensures the letter specifies clear deadlines for submitting the report, as well as guidelines for its structure and content. This ensures the CEO’s input is aligned with the broader appraisal process. The CEO’s summary report becomes a critical component of the performance review, offering a first-hand account of their work and serving as a foundation for discussions with the council and other stakeholders. This step ensures the appraisal is collaborative, balanced, and grounded in a comprehensive understanding of the CEO’s role.

3
CEO Review Data Requested from Finance

Blue Zoo collaborates with the local government’s finance department to collect key financial data relevant to the CEO’s performance appraisal. This step ensures the review incorporates objective metrics that reflect the CEO’s ability to manage the organisation’s financial health effectively. Financial data requested typically includes information on budget adherence, revenue generation, cost-saving measures, and the implementation of financial strategies that align with the council’s objectives. Blue Zoo ensures that the data collected from the finance team is accurate and comprehensive, providing a quantitative basis for evaluating the CEO’s performance. For example, if the CEO has successfully maintained fiscal discipline by delivering projects within budget or implementing innovative cost-reduction strategies, this information is documented as evidence of their financial stewardship. Conversely, any variances from budget projections are analysed to determine their underlying causes and whether they were within the CEO’s control. This step is critical for balancing qualitative feedback with measurable outcomes, ensuring the appraisal process remains fair and objective. Blue Zoo integrates the financial data with other inputs, such as staff and elected member feedback, to create a holistic assessment of the CEO’s performance. By grounding the review in concrete financial results, Blue Zoo ensures the appraisal accurately reflects the CEO’s contribution to achieving organisational sustainability and meeting community expectations.

4
Staff Survey Circulated with Focus on Behavioural Questions (if requested)

Blue Zoo develops and distributes a staff survey designed to gather confidential feedback on the CEO’s leadership and behavioural competencies. The survey focuses on key aspects of the CEO’s performance that directly impact the organisation’s culture and effectiveness, such as communication, decision-making, relationship-building, and fostering a positive work environment. The survey is carefully crafted to encourage honest and constructive feedback, with questions tailored to align with the council’s strategic priorities and the CEO’s role. For instance, staff may be asked to evaluate the CEO’s ability to inspire confidence, provide clear direction, and address organisational challenges effectively. Open- ended questions may also be included to allow staff to provide specific examples or elaborate on their responses. Blue Zoo ensures the survey is distributed in a manner that guarantees confidentiality, fostering a safe space for staff to share their insights without fear of reprisal. This step is essential for capturing the perspectives of those who work closely with the CEO on a daily basis, providing a grassroots view of their leadership impact. Once the survey responses are collected, Blue Zoo analyses the data to identify trends, patterns, and key themes. The results are used to provide a comprehensive assessment of the CEO’s behavioural performance, highlighting strengths and areas for improvement. By incorporating staff feedback into the appraisal process, Blue Zoo ensures the review is inclusive, well-rounded, and focused on continuous improvement.

5
Elected Member Survey Circulated That Includes KPI Results, CEO Comments, and Behavioural Questions

Blue Zoo designs and circulates a detailed survey to elected members, incorporating key performance indicators (KPIs), the CEO’s summary report, and behavioural assessment questions. This survey is tailored to evaluate the CEO’s performance from a governance perspective, focusing on how effectively they have delivered on strategic objectives, fulfilled council directives, and demonstrated leadership. The inclusion of KPI results ensures elected members can assess the CEO’s performance against measurable outcomes, such as project delivery, financial management, and community engagement. The CEO’s comments provide context for these results, helping elected members understand the rationale behind certain decisions or challenges. This promotes a fair and informed evaluation process. The behavioural questions focus on the CEO’s ability to work collaboratively with the council, foster trust, and maintain transparency in decision-making. Elected members may also be asked to assess how effectively the CEO has communicated with stakeholders, managed conflicts, and supported the council in achieving its strategic goals. Blue Zoo ensures the survey is easy to complete, providing clear instructions and examples where necessary. The responses are collected and analysed to provide insights into the CEO’s governance and leadership effectiveness. This step ensures the appraisal reflects the perspectives of those responsible for overseeing the CEO’s performance, providing critical input for the final evaluation.

6
Staff Survey Presented to Mayor Mayor for Approval (if requested) Before circulating the staff survey,

Blue Zoo presents the draft version to the Mayor for review and approval. This step ensures the survey aligns with the council’s expectations and focuses on the most relevant aspects of the CEO’s performance. Blue Zoo works closely with the Mayor to refine the survey, incorporating their input on the scope, language, and key areas of focus. The approval process ensures the survey captures meaningful feedback that contributes to a balanced and constructive performance appraisal. For instance, the Mayor may suggest additional questions or adjust the emphasis on certain topics, such as team-building or decision-making. This collaborative approach ensures the survey addresses both organisational and council priorities. Once the survey is approved, Blue Zoo prepares it for distribution, ensuring it is accessible and easy to complete.

7
Elected Member Survey Released to Elected Members

Blue Zoo releases the elected member survey to all council members after securing approval from the Mayor. This survey serves as a critical tool for gathering feedback on the CEO’s performance from the governance perspective. Blue Zoo ensures that the survey is distributed efficiently and that all elected members have sufficient time to provide thoughtful responses. The survey includes sections covering key performance indicators (KPIs), the CEO’s behavioural competencies, and their ability to deliver on strategic objectives. The inclusion of the CEO’s comments within the survey provides elected members with context for evaluating both achievements and challenges, promoting a balanced assessment. Blue Zoo ensures that the survey questions align with the council’s priorities, such as financial accountability, project delivery, community engagement, and leadership effectiveness. To encourage candid feedback, Blue Zoo emphasises the confidentiality of survey responses, allowing elected members to share their insights without concern for repercussions. The survey also includes open-ended questions to capture nuanced feedback and specific examples, offering richer insights into the CEO’s performance. Blue Zoo provides detailed instructions to ensure elected members understand the scope and intent of the survey, making it accessible and straightforward to complete. This step ensures that the perspectives of all elected members are incorporated into the performance appraisal, creating a comprehensive and well-rounded evaluation of the CEO’s effectiveness in their role.

8
Elected Member Survey Responses Collated and Analysed After the survey period closes,

Blue Zoo collects and analyses the responses from elected members. This step involves a systematic review of the data to identify key trends, themes, and outliers. Blue Zoo uses a combination of qualitative and quantitative analysis to ensure the feedback is accurate, balanced, and actionable. Quantitative data, such as ratings on specific questions, is aggregated to provide an overview of the CEO’s performance across different areas, such as leadership, strategic execution, and relationship management with the council. Qualitative feedback, including comments and suggestions, is reviewed to identify recurring themes and specific areas of concern or praise. For example, multiple comments about the CEO’s effective communication style or areas where transparency could be improved are flagged for further discussion. Blue Zoo ensures that the analysis remains objective and unbiased, presenting the data in a clear and structured manner. The results are synthesised into key insights that inform the final performance appraisal report. This analysis helps to highlight the CEO’s strengths and pinpoint areas for improvement, offering the council valuable input for evaluating the CEO’s overall effectiveness. By conducting a thorough review of the survey responses, Blue Zoo ensures the appraisal reflects the council’s collective perspective and provides actionable recommendations for future development.

Remuneration

Total Remuneration Package review

Review of Total Remuneration Package

1
Review the Current SAT Determination

Blue Zoo begins the Total Remuneration Package (TRP) review by examining the current Salaries and Allowances Tribunal (SAT) Determination. The SAT Determination sets the regulatory framework for determining the remuneration of local government CEOs in Western Australia. It outlines the remuneration bands and provides guidelines on the permissible components of the TRP, such as base salary, allowances, and non-monetary benefits. This step ensures that the TRP review adheres to legal requirements and provides a structured benchmark for assessing the CEO’s remuneration. Blue Zoo carefully reviews the latest SAT Determination to ensure the most up-to-date and relevant guidance is applied. This involves understanding the criteria used to classify local governments into bands and the parameters for what constitutes allowable remuneration. The review of the SAT Determination also helps Blue Zoo identify opportunities or constraints within the framework. For example, the SAT Determination may allow certain discretionary allowances under specific circumstances, which could be applicable to the CEO’s role. By thoroughly understanding these guidelines, Blue Zoo ensures that the review is both compliant and flexible enough to consider unique organisational needs. This foundational step provides a clear reference point for evaluating the CEO’s current TRP against SAT regulations, ensuring all subsequent steps align with the statutory framework. It also sets the stage for comparing the CEO’s remuneration to the SAT band classification applicable to the local government.

2
Confirm the Classification of the Town Under the SAT Determination

Blue Zoo confirms the classification of the local government under the SAT Determination, as this classification dictates the applicable remuneration band for the CEO. The classification is based on a set of criteria determined by the SAT, including factors such as the local government’s population size, geographical area, operational complexity, and annual budget. These criteria reflect the scale and responsibilities associated with the CEO’s role, ensuring that the TRP is appropriate for the specific demands of the position. This step ensures that Blue Zoo evaluates the CEO’s remuneration package against the correct benchmark. Misclassification could result in non-compliance with SAT guidelines or an inappropriate TRP, either exceeding the allowable band or failing to meet competitive standards. Blue Zoo carefully reviews the town’s classification data, comparing it against the SAT’s criteria to confirm that the CEO’s role is accurately represented. Additionally, Blue Zoo ensures that the classification reflects the most current organisational and community context. For instance, changes in population size, operational scope, or financial resources may require an updated classification or adjustments to the TRP.
