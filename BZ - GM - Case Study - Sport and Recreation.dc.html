The challenge

The sport and recreation industry in Western Australia comprises more than 200,000 volunteers and employees who enable more than 5,000 clubs to deliver programs at a community level. This local system is supported by a network of regional and state associations and peak bodies.

Sport 4 All was a four‑year, $20 million project to increase participation in community sport and recreation throughout Western Australia. Its ClubTalk component provided state sporting associations, peak bodies and clubs with the knowledge to use information and communications technology to make running a club easier and more cost effective.

Using Governance Manager

The Western Australian Department of Sport and Recreation used Governance Manager in partnership with all 93 sporting associations to assess, compare and improve ICT capacity across the sector — informing the discharge of a four‑year, multi‑million dollar grant funding program.

Through ClubTalk, the Department committed to ensuring every not‑for‑profit recreation and state sporting association and their clubs had a basic level of ICT capacity and access to expertise. Associations including Netball, Rugby and the WA Football Commission participated in benchmarking their ICT capability maturity using Governance Manager, informing recommendations on the investment and development of innovative, cost‑effective ICT.

Outcome achieved

The assessment was conducted using the Small Business ICT Governance Framework, which uses capability maturity models to support objective measurement across a level 1 to 4 scale. Importantly, a level 4 is not inherently good and a level 1 not inherently bad — governance is about finding the right level for each organisation's needs, size and architecture.

“Governance Manager was used by the Department of Sport and Recreation to benchmark the ICT governance capability of the state associations and peak bodies overseeing the 5,000 clubs in Western Australia. The unparalleled insight into the governance of ICT in the sector was used as an aid to discharge $4 million in ICT funding.”
