How this playbook works

Solve problems agencies already have

The strongest campaigns for the WA public sector solve a problem an agency already recognises, rather than promoting governance as an abstract idea. Every campaign is built around a practical question that a Board member, Director General, executive or internal auditor is already asking.

1
person on marketing
1 day
per week of capacity
$0
external budget — internal time & systems only

Operating rules

  • One campaign at a time. Run in sequence, one per quarter — never in parallel.
  • Build once, reuse everywhere. One anchor asset feeds the article series and emails.
  • Lead with a free assessment. Every campaign ends in a Governance Manager tool, not a sales call.
  • Measure, then decide. Keep what earns engagement; drop what doesn't.

Channels we own (no spend)

  • LinkedIn — organic articles & video series
  • Email / EDM to the existing list
  • Insight articles — published on our channels
  • Governance Manager — assessments & benchmarks
  • Website — downloadable resources

Who does what

MarketingPlan, produce, publish, report
ConsultantsSubject content, insight articles, debriefs
ProductGovernance Manager modules & tools
LeadershipApprove messaging, follow up leads

Reporting cadence

  • Weekly — publish, respond, note what's landing
  • Monthly — the four numbers to leadership
  • Quarterly — campaign wrap & decide what to keep

Brand guardrails

  • Voice: calm, senior, plain-spoken. Advisor in the room.
  • Sentence case for headings; AU spelling; no emoji.
  • One service colour owns each campaign — never mix.
  • Lead with the outcome, then the method.
Government executives rarely want to buy consulting.
They want to avoid adverse findings, govern AI well, and show Directors General and Ministers evidence. Every campaign starts there.
