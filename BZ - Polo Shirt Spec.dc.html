Polo shirt

1  Overview & intent

This polo is a staff and partner garment — worn to client sites, events and everyday office wear. It should read instantly as Blue Zoo, feel premium and well‑made, and hold its shape and colour through repeated wear. The design is deliberately understated: deep navy, with the all‑white butterfly mark on the left chest and a single quiet line of copy across the rear shoulder blades.

The mark is used exactly as supplied — the origami butterfly, all‑white version, symbol only, no wordmark. Proportions must not be altered. This document is the single source of truth for the manufacturer; anything not drawn here is to be confirmed with Blue Zoo before production.

2  Construction & fit

A classic short‑sleeve pique polo with a ribbed collar, three‑button placket and a regular fit.

  • Fabric. Combed cotton pique, ~210 gsm, with a soft handle and a firm, breathable knit; pre‑shrunk and colourfast. Regular fit; hip length with a subtle side vent.
  • Collar & placket. Flat‑knit ribbed collar and cuffs in tonal navy; three‑button placket with tonal navy buttons; clean twin‑needle topstitching.
  • Sleeves & hem. Set‑in short sleeves with ribbed cuffs; straight hem with side vents; reinforced shoulder taping for shape retention.
  • Branding. Embroidered all‑white mark on the left chest; the rear line applied across the shoulder blades (see Section 4).

4  Colour & branding

The body is deep Blue Zoo navy. Both the chest mark and the rear line are rendered in all white for clean contrast against the navy — use the supplied all‑white butterfly mark, not the colour version. The rear line reads Governance. Done Differently. set in the brand face, bold, centred across the shoulder blades.

5  Labelling & packaging

  • Neck label woven with the lowercase bluezoo wordmark, or a soft‑print tagless equivalent for comfort.
  • Care label in the left side seam: fibre content, size and wash care to AU standards.
  • Sizing XS–3XL to a Blue Zoo‑approved size chart; supply a full size set for fit sign‑off.
  • Packaging individually folded in a recyclable poly bag with a size sticker; bulk‑packed by size.

Alternatives worth a sample: (a) cotton‑rich moisture‑wicking blend; (b) rear line as embroidery vs soft‑hand print; (c) a slim‑fit option alongside regular.
