Follow-up email — peak bodies & sector regulators

Sent 7–10 business days after the intro if there is no reply. Gives the four sector questions as standalone value, then lowers the ask.

Governance done differently
Subject: Re: A governance benchmark for [sector] — four questions instead
From: Pat Scally <pats@bluezoo.com.au>
To: [First name] [Last name], [Peak body]

Dear [First name],

Following up briefly on my note about a sector governance benchmark. Rather than ask again, here are the four questions a peak body can only answer with aggregate data — useful to sit with, whether or not we ever speak:

  1. The benchmark. If a minister asked you how well [sector] is governed, what would you show them?
  2. The common weakness. Which governance domain is weakest across most of your members — and is that where your development effort is currently aimed?
  3. The pockets of excellence. Which members are genuinely strong in a domain where others struggle, and is their approach being shared with the rest?
  4. The early warning. If a member's governance were deteriorating, how would you learn of it — before an incident rather than after?

Question 3 tends to be the one that stays with people. In every sector assessment we have run, capability already exists somewhere in the membership — the peak body simply had no way of knowing where. Connecting the strong to the struggling is the cheapest sector improvement available, and it is invisible without measurement.

Two easy options, take either:

  • Send me the aggregator overview and I'll leave you to it — just reply “send it”.
  • Scope the free pilot — one framework domain, up to ten volunteer members, an anonymised snapshot for your board.

And if this isn't the right year for it, please just say so and I'll close the loop — no further follow‑ups.
