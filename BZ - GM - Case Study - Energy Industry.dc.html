The challenge

A major electricity generator and retailer with reasonably established governance controls and management capabilities recognised it needed to improve enterprise risk management and business continuity capability. Weaknesses were evident, and past efforts to improve had been patchy — driven mostly by diligent individuals within disparate areas, in reaction to incidents and audits, and lacking the top leadership buy‑in required to create sustainable change. The Board and executive were committed to a holistic approach.

Using Governance Manager

Governance Manager was adopted to benchmark the maturity of the organisation's capability against ISO 31000 and ISO 22301, providing a baseline for measuring future improvement. All senior managers and functional leaders were involved in workshops to score the current state, with further workshops held with the executive to set future‑state goals guiding a three‑year capability improvement program. Conversations and supporting documents were captured to support decision traceability.

With the gaps between current and future state agreed, Governance Manager automatically generated a recommended program of activities. Accountable teams selected what to include in the long‑term plan and added activities specific to their context. The multi‑million dollar remediation program was approved readily on the strength of the strategic assessment, widespread stakeholder buy‑in and clear alignment with organisational requirements — then managed and tracked through the platform.

Outcome achieved

Every six months the assessment is conducted again to evaluate improvement and ensure the goals remain relevant. The process has since been repeated for additional frameworks — occupational health and safety, corporate governance and environmental management — building similar roadmaps.

The federated assessment functionality is now used to assess and compare capability across each of the power stations.

Applicable where improvement effort has been reactive and individually driven, and the board wants a holistic roadmap with an evidenced investment case.
