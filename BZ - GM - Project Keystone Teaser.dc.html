The opportunity

A ready-built governance methodology, for sale

A wholly owned, cloud-based software-as-a-service platform is being offered for sale. The platform helps organisations measure, manage and improve governance and capability maturity across many disciplines — converting subjective governance review into structured, repeatable, evidence-based assessment, supported by an extensive framework library, benchmarking, improvement planning, reporting and a substantial video learning library.

The asset is established, proven and revenue-generating, with a loyal base of government and corporate clients across Australia — several on multi-year, “sticky” contracts and some engaged via a partner network. It is offered with the option to retain its long-serving lead developer to ensure continuity.

The vendor is divesting because the product sits outside its core advisory business. For a consulting or audit firm, it is a rare chance to acquire a defensible methodology and a recurring software revenue line deployable across an existing client base from day one.
