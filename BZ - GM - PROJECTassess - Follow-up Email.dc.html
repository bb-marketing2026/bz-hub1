Following up briefly on my note about assessing one domain of your project governance. Rather than ask again, here are the five questions PROJECTassess actually puts to a project — useful on their own, whether or not we ever speak:

  1. Framework. Are roles, responsibilities and decision rights defined and written down — and would two people describe them the same way?
  2. Strategic alignment. Can you show how this project was selected and prioritised against strategic value, not just approved?
  3. Resource management. Are resource commitments across competing projects tracked, or agreed verbally and discovered later?
  4. Stakeholder communication. Does your steering committee make decisions on the record, or receive status and move on?
  5. Compliance monitoring. Is compliance tracked through the lifecycle, or assembled in the fortnight before a review?

If any of those gave you pause, that is the gap a gateway or audit review will find first — and the domain worth assessing first. Pick one and we'll measure just that.

Two easy options, take either:

  • Send me the framework overview and I'll leave you to it — just reply “send it”.
  • Book the free assessment on whichever single domain concerns you most, with a 30‑minute debrief.

And if project governance simply isn't your priority this quarter, say so and I'll close the loop — no follow‑ups.
