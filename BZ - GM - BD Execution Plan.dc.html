Orientation

How to use this plan

This is the operating manual for the Governance Manager growth program’s first year. It assumes the program is led by an incoming business development lead who is new to bluezoo and the product, and it takes the program from learning the product to fully embedded in a target market — week by week, task by task. It is deliberately prescriptive in the early weeks and increasingly outcome-led as the team builds judgement. The plan runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027; the four quarterly gates fall at the end of September 2026, December 2026, March 2027 and June 2027.

  • Work the plan in order. Each week lists tasks, the output it should produce, and the KPI it advances.
  • The 52 weeks are grouped into five phases. Each phase opens with its objectives, the KPIs in focus, and the exit criteria to hit before moving on.
  • Treat the weekly targets (calls, demos, proposals, signings) as commitments, not suggestions. They ladder directly to the annual KPIs.
  • If the program falls behind, it does not silently slip — the gap is raised at the weekly check-in and re-planned with the sponsor.

The program’s sponsor is Tony Friday (Director) — the route to product history, client introductions and sign-off on pricing, partnering and the sale narrative. Both the business development lead and the offshore BDM are recruited during the first six weeks and start in Week 7. Weeks 1–6 are therefore a mobilisation window led by Pat (a local Australian executive and product leader; no incremental cost, ~4 hours/week) with Tony: they ready the product knowledge, data, HubSpot and target lists, and run the hiring — Pat sits on the interview panel in Weeks 3–4. From Week 7 the BD lead runs the program day to day and directs the BDM, who owns international distributor recruitment; Pat continues throughout for local demonstrations and product authority.

The program’s mandate

The program & what success looks like

The program owns the commercial growth of Governance Manager and its readiness for sale: treble revenue and, just as importantly, build durable recurring revenue and repeatable go-to-market systems that survive a change of ownership. It sells through three motions — direct, partner and international distribution. From Week 7 a business development lead runs the program day to day and directs the offshore BDM; both are recruited in Weeks 1–6, a mobilisation window led by Pat and Tony. Pat, a local Australian executive and product leader (no incremental cost, ~4 hours/week), provides in-person demonstrations and product authority in Australia throughout.

Ways of working

Operating rhythm

A predictable cadence keeps the plan honest. Hold these rhythms from week one; they are how the program stays ahead of the KPIs rather than reporting on them after the fact.

Daily

Work the pipeline: outreach, follow-ups, demos and proposal progression. Update the CRM same-day. Unblock the BDM.

Weekly

30-minute check-in with Tony: pipeline movement, wins, blockers, week-ahead plan. BDM 1:1. Refresh the KPI tracker.

Monthly

Full KPI review vs glidepath; forecast for the quarter; spend check against the A$75k marketing envelope; pipeline-coverage review.

Quarterly

Phase gate: review against exit criteria, reallocate effort to the best-performing channel, and lock the next quarter’s plan.

Deploying Pat — ~4 hrs/week, no cost

Pat is a local Australian executive and product leader whose salary sits elsewhere, so his time is free to the plan. Spend his four hours a week where in-person product authority converts best: local demonstrations, whole-of-sector and government meetings, large-client value reviews, partner product training and conference presence. Book him a week ahead against the highest-value opportunity, and lean on him for product leadership while the BD lead builds fluency. In Weeks 1–6, before the BDM starts, Pat is the program’s main in-field resource alongside the BD lead. Pat already holds a strong relationship with the largest client and will visit them on-site in July and November — anchor that client’s renewal and expansion around those two visits.
