01 · The objective

From steady to scaled

Governance Manager is a proven, profitable platform that has grown modestly — FY26 revenue of A$239,141 from a small client base, despite case studies that demonstrate it can run at whole-of-sector scale. The opportunity is to convert that latent potential into momentum: a focused, aggressive 12-month push to treble revenue to approximately A$717,000 across both direct and partner channels, lifting recurring revenue and building a materially stronger, more scalable business.

This plan sets out the growth model, the five pillars that drive it, a quarter-by-quarter activity roadmap, and a fully costed investment of A$159,000 — a lean A$75,000 of marketing and enablement plus two dedicated offshore business development managers (A$84,000 combined) tasked with extending Governance Manager’s market presence in any jurisdiction. The model is deliberately channel-led: partners and international distributors carry the cost of in-market selling, funded through margin rather than fixed budget, so the plan still targets a trebling of revenue on a modest outlay.

02 · The growth model

Where the trebling comes from

Trebling is not achieved through one channel. It is the sum of five compounding levers, weighted toward the scalable channels the case studies already prove — whole-of-sector programs and reseller networks, now extended internationally — rather than slow one-by-one direct sales.

03 · Strategy

Five pillars of growth

1 · Whole-of-sector & government programs
+$150k

Replicate the NDS and Sport 4 All model: sell once to a peak body or department and deploy across dozens of member entities, with whole-of-sector benchmarking. Highest-value, most defensible revenue. Target three new programs across disability, sport/recreation, local government, health and not-for-profit sectors.

2 · Domestic partner & reseller channel
+$144k

Recruit and enable consulting and audit firms to resell assessments under a formal partner program — deal registration, margin share and co-marketing support. Each partner becomes a multiplier, embedding the platform in their own client engagements. Grow from one reseller to six active partners.

3 · International software distributors
+$45k

Appoint software distributors and resellers in priority overseas markets — starting with aligned, English-speaking jurisdictions (New Zealand, the UK, Ireland, Canada) and selected regional hubs (Singapore, the UAE). Distributors localise, sell and support in-market in exchange for margin, so reach scales with little fixed cost. Two dedicated offshore business development managers own this push across any jurisdiction. Seed two to three agreements in year one, with substantial upside in the years beyond.

4 · Productised direct demand generation
+$84k

Turn the platform into an inbound engine: clear packaging and pricing, SEO and thought-leadership content, paid search and LinkedIn ABM, and a self-serve demo/trial. Grow assessment-only clients from eight to roughly forty-five.

5 · Expand & retain existing clients
+$55k

Land-and-expand the two large clients with additional frameworks, business units and users, and lock in multi-year renewals to strengthen the recurring base. Lowest-cost, lowest-risk revenue.

04 · Execution

12-month activity roadmap

Q1
Mo 1–3
Foundations

Recruit and onboard both offshore business development managers; stand up CRM and marketing automation; finalise the partner program, pricing and co-marketing offers; build the international distributor proposition and target list; package existing case studies; build initial pipeline from existing partner and sector contacts.

Q2
Mo 4–6
Launch

Go live with paid media and ABM; sign the first domestic partners; open peak-body and government outreach; the BDMs begin international distributor conversations; launch the self-serve demo; progress and close the first new sector program.

Q3
Mo 7–9
Scale

Grow the domestic channel to six active partners and drive assessment volume; sign the first two to three international distributors; close the second and third sector programs; attend priority sector conferences; expand the two large clients.

Q4
Mo 10–12
Convert & consolidate

Convert the pipeline and onboard international distributors; secure multi-year renewals to maximise recurring mix; document the repeatable sales, partner and distributor playbook; consolidate the growth narrative and updated metrics into the standing performance pack.

07 · The investment case

Why the spend pays back

At A$159,000 — A$75,000 of marketing and enablement plus A$84,000 for two offshore BDMs — the program is comfortably self-funding within the year: the incremental gross profit on ~A$478k of new revenue far exceeds the spend, because partners and distributors carry the cost of in-market selling. The strategic return is a structurally stronger business: trebled revenue, a materially higher recurring share, and an international distribution footprint that keeps compounding beyond the twelve months.
