Casual jacket

A premium, smart‑casual full‑zip jacket in deep Blue Zoo navy, carrying the origami butterfly mark embroidered on the left chest. Cut for the boardroom‑to‑client day: clean lines, a soft point collar and a comfortable regular fit. Understated, considered, unmistakably us.

1  Overview & intent

This jacket is a staff and partner garment — worn over a business shirt for client visits, events and everyday office wear. It should read instantly as Blue Zoo, feel premium and well‑made, and sit comfortably across a range of builds. The design is deliberately understated: a clean, smart‑casual full‑zip in deep navy, with the origami butterfly mark embroidered discreetly on the left chest — no large prints, no loud branding.

The mark is used exactly as supplied — the origami butterfly, symbol only, no wordmark. The facet geometry, the navy/teal split and the relative proportions must not be altered. This document is the single source of truth for the manufacturer; anything not drawn here is to be confirmed with Blue Zoo before production.

2  Construction & fit

A lined, full‑zip casual jacket with a soft point collar, set‑in sleeves and a straight hem finishing at the hip.

  • Shell. Mid‑weight poly‑viscose twill (~240 gsm) with a soft handle and a subtle sheen; wrinkle‑resistant and easy‑care. Regular fit with a slight taper; hip length.
  • Front. Full‑length centre‑front zip (YKK or equal) with a matching navy tape and a metal puller; two side‑seam welt pockets with zip closure; one internal chest pocket.
  • Collar & cuffs. Soft point collar; set‑in sleeves with plain, elasticated‑free cuffs; straight hem, both finished with clean topstitching.
  • Lining. Full smooth polyester lining in tonal navy for easy layering over a shirt.

Poly‑viscose twill is specified for a premium handle that holds colour and shape through repeated wear and wash. A wool‑blend upgrade can be sampled if a more formal handle is preferred.

4  Colour & branding

The shell is deep Blue Zoo navy. The embroidered mark uses the two brand hues — deep navy and teal — with a fine silver‑grey outline thread standing in for the metal facet ridges. Match thread to Pantone; approve a stitched‑out sample before bulk.

The mark stays the only branding on the garment face; do not add printed logos, back prints or sleeve badges. Keep the embroidered facets solid — do not blend thread colours within a facet.

5  Labelling & packaging

  • Neck label woven with the lowercase bluezoo wordmark, centred at the inside back neck.
  • Care label in the left side seam: fibre content, size and wash care to AU standards.
  • Sizing XS–3XL to a Blue Zoo‑approved size chart; supply a full size set for fit sign‑off.
  • Packaging individually folded in a recyclable poly bag with a size sticker; bulk‑packed by size.

Open items & assumptions

Shell (poly‑viscose twill, navy), fit (regular, hip length), branding (embroidered left‑chest mark) and sizing (XS–3XL) are proposed defaults — please confirm or amend. Alternatives worth a sample: (a) wool‑blend shell for a more formal handle; (b) a subtle tonal mark for an even quieter look; (c) an added embroidered bluezoo wordmark beneath the mark.
