About bluezoo & Governance Manager

bluezoo is a recognised international governance firm. Our software platform, Governance Manager, helps organisations measure, manage and improve governance and capability maturity — with an extensive library of frameworks, benchmarking, improvement planning and reporting. It is established, proven and trusted by government and corporate clients, and has been deployed at whole-of-sector scale.

We are entering an ambitious growth phase and are looking for a driven Business Development Manager to take the lead on sales worldwide.

The role

Own the growth of a proven product

As our sales lead, you will own the commercial growth of Governance Manager end to end. You will sell through three motions — direct clients, channel partners (consulting and audit firms), and international software distributors — supported by local Australian product leadership for demonstrations and technical depth. This is a hands-on, target-driven role with real ownership and a clear growth mandate.

  • Build and convert a qualified pipeline across direct, partner and distributor channels.
  • Run discovery and product demonstrations, prepare proposals, negotiate and close.
  • Recruit, enable and manage channel partners and international distributors.
  • Pursue whole-of-sector and government programs, and expand existing accounts into multi-year, recurring contracts.
  • Maintain disciplined CRM hygiene, accurate forecasting and KPI reporting (HubSpot).
  • Work closely with the Director and product leadership to hit aggressive growth targets.
Essential experience

You must have direct, recent and verifiable experience selling software (SaaS) solutions to Australian and/or US distributors, partners and/or direct clients. This is a non-negotiable requirement; applications will be assessed on demonstrable, checkable results.

  • A proven track record of meeting or exceeding B2B software sales targets.
  • Experience building and managing partner, reseller or distributor channels.
  • Full-cycle sales capability: prospecting, qualification, demonstration, proposal, negotiation and close.
  • Excellent written and spoken English, and confidence engaging Australian and US executives.
  • Strong CRM discipline (HubSpot or equivalent) and accurate forecasting.
  • A self-managed remote worker able to maintain a working-hours overlap with Australian business hours.
Also valuable
  • Governance, risk, compliance or audit domain knowledge.
  • Selling into government and not-for-profit sectors.
  • Whole-of-sector or enterprise deal experience.
  • An existing Australian and/or US network.
