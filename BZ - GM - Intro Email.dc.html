Most governance managers I speak with can describe how their organisation is governed with confidence — but find it much harder to measure it, or to show the board an objective picture of where the gaps are and whether things are improving year on year.

That's the problem we built Governance Manager to solve. It's a software platform that turns governance from opinion into evidence: you assess your practices against recognised frameworks and international standards, and it produces maturity scores, pragmatic recommendations to close the gaps, and time-series and benchmark reports you can take straight to your board or audit committee.

For a team your size it's genuinely practical — the assessments are guided and repeatable, so you're not starting from a blank page, and you can focus finite resources on the few practices that will make the biggest difference next.

A few things it's useful for:

  • Getting a defensible baseline of governance maturity across the organisation
  • Showing measurable progress to the board, not just activity
  • Benchmarking quietly against comparable organisations
  • Turning findings into a prioritised improvement plan you can track
