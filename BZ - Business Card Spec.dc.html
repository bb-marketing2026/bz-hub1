1  Overview & intent

The card is the firm's most personal piece of collateral. It should feel weighty and considered in the hand and read instantly as Blue Zoo. The front carries the wordmark, the holder's name in silver foil and a blind‑deboss butterfly; the reverse is a clean white card with the primary lockup, the brand line and full contact details. The design is deliberately restrained — one foil, one deboss, generous space.

2  Layout & dimensions

  • Trim size. 90 × 55 mm (landscape), square corners.
  • Bleed. 3 mm on every side — artwork size 96 × 61 mm. The navy front background must extend to full bleed.
  • Safe area. Keep all text and the logo at least 4 mm inside the trim.
  • Contact block (rear). pats@bluezoo.com.au · +61 8 9370 0409 · bluezoo.com.au · 896 Beaufort Street, Inglewood WA 6052.

5  Artwork layers for the printer

Supply print‑ready PDF/X‑1a with each special finish on its own named spot layer (100% coverage, overprint on):

  • CMYK — front navy flood & all back artwork.
  • FOIL‑SILVER — name and short rule (spot, e.g. Pantone 877).
  • DEBOSS — butterfly outline (spot, marked "blind deboss, no ink").
  • DIELINE — trim path (spot, marked non‑printing).
Fonts outlined; images embedded at 300 dpi; total ink coverage ≤ 300%; rich black for the navy flood built as C60 M40 K100. A separate print‑marks artboard (BZ‑BC‑001‑PRINT) accompanies this spec with trim, bleed, registration marks and colour bars in place.
