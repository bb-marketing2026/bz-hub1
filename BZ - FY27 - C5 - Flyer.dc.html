CEO Performance Appraisal

An independent, statutory-compliant review of your Chief Executive Officer — run end to end, on the record, and built to be defensible.

Under the Local Government Act 1995, every council must review its CEO's performance each year. Blue Zoo delivers that review to best practice — independent of the CEO and any single councillor, aligned to the sector's minimum standards, and benchmarked against the Salaries and Allowances Tribunal's Elected Member and CEO Remuneration Determination.
