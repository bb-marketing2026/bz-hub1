To be clear at the outset: this isn't a valuation service, and I'm not suggesting anything is wrong with your numbers.

What I'd like to raise is the process that produces them. When a buyer's adviser, an auditor or a regulator interrogates a valuation, they rarely start with the answer — they start with the methodology. Which approach was applied, and why. Whether it was applied consistently across comparable assets and business units. Who owns the key assumptions, and where they're documented. What independent review the model received before the number left finance.

Most finance leaders are confident their valuations are sound. Far fewer can produce a governed, documented account of the process behind them — because valuation governance is rarely measured as a discipline in its own right.

The VALassess Framework in Governance Manager assesses exactly that, across four capabilities: standardisation of methodology, regulatory and reporting compliance, integration of the full spectrum of value factors including ESG, and the capability of the people doing the work. The output is a maturity profile with the gaps ranked. You don't have to take on all four — pick the one domain you're least comfortable about and we'll assess just that.

I'd like to offer you a free assessment against one domain of the framework — whichever you choose. Practically:

  • Under an hour of your time — no model access, no data extracts
  • A maturity profile on that domain and a prioritised gap list, in writing
  • A 30‑minute debrief with me to talk through what it shows
  • The result is yours — nothing goes to your board or auditors unless you send it

Two points where it tends to earn its hour quickly: the next fair‑value or impairment conversation with your auditors, and any transaction or capital raise where diligence will test the methodology before it accepts the number.

Worth a short call to see whether it's useful? If the timing's wrong, I'm happy to just send the framework overview so you have it before year‑end.
