The maturity model — used at steps 4, 5 and 6
1Initial — informal, person-dependent
2Developing — exists, applied inconsistently
3Established — documented and evidenced
4Managed — monitored, improving
5Leading — embedded, benchmarked
Why the order matters

Read the benchmark before scoring. Knowing where the sector sits stops a comfortable target being set by default — and shows whether a desired state is genuinely ambitious.

The two loops

Inner loop — return to step 3 and score the next element, until all three in the principle are done.

Outer loop — return to step 2 and take the next principle, until all three are complete.

Step 7 — what the reports give you
▪ Maturity profile across all nine elements
▪ Ranked gap list, current to desired
▪ Improvement roadmap with owners and dates
▪ Board dashboard and benchmark comparison
